Features

Built for trust and speed

AI-powered matching

Visual and semantic analysis compares descriptions, photos, and context to find your item across the entire network with high precision.

Global network

Your lost item is searched across individuals, transport companies, airports, and venues — not just nearby posts. One report reaches everywhere.

Secure & private

Chat only unlocks after a verified match. Your personal information stays protected. Ownership verification built in.

Built-in rewards

Set a reward for your lost item. Finders — whether individuals or organizations — get compensated for doing the right thing.
